  Get ready for Halloween with a Clothing Swap!

  Bring **clean** and dry clothing in good condition and swap for new to you clothing. Please avoid bringing clothing with strong scents, excessive pet fur, or other allergens.   If you are bringing clothing to swap, please bring it before 2:00 pm. After 2:00 pm, we invite any community members or organizations to take as much as they will use.

  Schedule:

  * 10:00 am - Clothing Swap begins
  * 11:00 pm - 2:00 pm - Mending Station
  * 2:00 pm - Take what you'll use
  * 3:00 pm - Clothing Swap Ends

  Mending Station: Do you have clothing that needs to be mended? We'll have a mending station set up with experts who can help you troubleshoot how to mend your items and potentially fix them on the spot.
