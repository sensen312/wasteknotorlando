  Celebrate Good Neighbor Week with Audubon Park Garden District

  Buy Nothing Swap!

  How does this work? Take all you want, give all you want. You don’t need to trade, barter, ask permission, feel weird, or anything. You do not have to bring something to take something. Just come as you are, and have fun! 

  If you bring things to give away, you must take any leftovers with you when you leave. 

  You can set up on a table inside or bring a blanket and set up outside

  You can bring ALMOST anything to this event. We just ask that you don’t bring really big items (like furniture) and nothing gross (like dirty underwear or hazardous chemicals). 

  Do I have to stay the whole time? Your participation is entirely up to you. If you brought some items and they were not taken by the time you want to leave, please take them back with you. If you are able to help clean up at the end, please do!! Just show up and help. The more people, the easier it is for all of us!
